How the calculation works

The calculator figures out what payment adjustments are needed when a suspension is added to a membership. It compares what the member has already paid against what they owe for the period around the suspension.

Key concepts
Overpaid days
Days the member already paid for that fall after the suspension starts. These days are now covered by the suspension, so the member is ahead.
Gap days
Days between the suspension end and the next scheduled payment date. The member hasn't paid for these yet and will owe them when they return.
The four outcomes
  1. Suspension fits within one billing period (no payments to delete)
    Simply reduce the next payment by the prorated suspension value.
  2. Overpaid = Gap
    The credit and the debt cancel out exactly. Just delete the payments that fall within the suspension — no money adjustment needed.
  3. Overpaid > Gap (member has a net credit)
    Delete suspended payments and reduce the next scheduled payment by the difference.
  4. Overpaid < Gap (member owes extra)
    Delete suspended payments and add a one-off catch-up payment for the shortfall before regular billing resumes.
Prorating

All amounts are prorated by day. The period length is 7 days (weekly), 14 days (fortnightly), or the number of days in the billing month (monthly).
